How this website handles information when you visit.

Last updated October 23, 2026

This is Scott VanDeWater’s personal professional website. This notice explains the services involved when you visit and the information they may receive.

The site has no visitor accounts, contact forms, or newsletter signup.

Google Analytics

I use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this website. It uses cookies and similar identifiers to measure visits. Depending on your browser and Analytics settings, information can include pages visited, referral information, browser and device details, and approximate location.

Analytics currently loads automatically when a page opens. This website does not currently present an analytics consent prompt. Google processes the information under its own policies.

GitHub Pages hosting

GitHub Pages hosts and delivers this website. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed into GitHub.

Fonts

The typefaces are served from this website alongside its page assets. Loading them does not require a connection to Google Fonts.

Your choices

You can use your browser’s controls to manage or delete cookies and block tracking requests. Google also provides an Analytics opt-out browser add-on. Opting out of Analytics does not prevent the requests needed to deliver this website.
