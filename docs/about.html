About Me

I've been interested in building things and solving problems for as long as I can remember.

As a kid, I was repairing bicycles, experimenting with small business ventures, and learning to program on an Apple IIc. This was before the internet, so when I got stuck with Apple BASIC, I'd write questions to one of my mother's coworkers and wait for a response.

I've always enjoyed figuring things out, whether there's a practical reason or simply a challenge I want to solve.

That interest eventually became a career spanning software development, architecture, entrepreneurship, and leadership. I've had the opportunity to build businesses, develop technology, and work with some great people along the way. I still enjoy getting into the technical details as much as thinking about the bigger picture.

Not everything that shaped me came from work.

A significant childhood illness disrupted many of my early friendships and relationships. As I got older, I made a conscious effort to better understand people, their motivations, and how they see the world. It didn't always come naturally, and it's something I continue to work at.

Loyalty, understanding, and contribution are particularly important to me. I take relationships and commitments seriously, and I find a great deal of satisfaction in being helpful. Sometimes that's solving a technical problem or working through a business challenge. Other times it's helping someone accomplish something they've been working toward. Seeing someone succeed and knowing I played a part in getting them there is especially rewarding.

Outside of work, I enjoy golf, gaming, and spending time with my family. We're usually finding something to do on the weekends, whether that's a Renaissance festival, Comic Con, an apple orchard, or exploring somewhere new around Michigan.

And when I have some downtime, there's a good chance I'm working through a math problem, exploring a business idea, or trying to solve a puzzle simply because it's interesting.
